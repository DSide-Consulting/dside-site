What it is

Back pain isn't one thing. Neither is PT.

There's more than one approach to back pain with real evidence behind it. McKenzie. McGill. Sahrmann. Manual therapy. They work best on different people.

Most people never hear that. They get sent to "PT" and land wherever's closest.

STAN asks about your back. Figures out which approach fits you. Then shows you a few real NYC physical therapists who work that way, with the trade-offs of each and how to book.

What I'm asking

About 10 minutes. Start to finish.

  • Go all the way through. Questions, your result, the provider options.
  • Tell me where you got stuck. Confusing questions, slow screens, anything that made you want to quit.
  • Tell me if you'd book. Did the options make sense? Would you trust them?
Use your real back or a made-up one. Your call. It's a test version, so if you'd rather not enter your own health info, invent it.
Fair warning

It's early.

The providers come from the public national provider registry. They haven't signed up for STAN yet, so some won't have booking links or availability.

STAN isn't a diagnosis. If something feels urgent, call your doctor.
